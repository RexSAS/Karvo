Log your ski lessons in seconds and always know your hours and what you're owed.
---
Karvo is the lesson log for ski instructors.

You're paid by the hour, and at the end of the month the hours have to add up. With Karvo you log each lesson the moment it ends, then see your month, your season and your pay at a glance. No account, no sign-up, nothing to set up.

LOG A LESSON IN SECONDS
• Quick durations (1h, 1h 30m, 2h, 3h) or type your own
• Student names suggested from your past lessons
• A note for each lesson, like "Kids 6 and 9" or "Lesson in English"
• Lessons grouped by month, with each month's total
• Search by student or note, and show only the lessons not paid yet

YOUR HOURS AND YOUR PAY
• Your season's hours, compared with last season at the same date
• Look back at any past season, or at everything you've ever taught
• Earnings from your hourly rates, hidden until you tap the eye, with fingerprint, face or PIN if you want
• Tick the months the school has paid, and see what is still to be paid
• Statistics: lessons this month, average lesson, students, the last 6 months, your busiest weekdays and a podium of your top students
• Tap a student for their own page: their hours, lessons, months and seasons

SEASON BADGES
• 14 badges unlock as your season's hours grow, from Karver at your first hour to Legend of the slopes at 600h
• Bronze, silver, gold, amethyst and ice, each new one with a notification and an unlock animation
• See your progress to the next badge, and your best badge of every season

HOME-SCREEN WIDGETS
• This week and This month: your hours and lessons, with a + to add a lesson
• Overview: the week, the month, today, the season and your next event
• This week vs last: both weeks' hours, day by day

CALENDAR AND REMINDERS
• Add events to any day, like "Ski school meeting 17:00"
• Karvo reminds you at 07:30 on the day
• The next 3 days' events right on the Lessons page
• On the first day of the season, Kevin wishes you a good one, and Karvo sums up the season before

MOMENTS
• Tap for a photo, hold for a video of up to 10 seconds: up to 3 moments a day
• Your season as a photo calendar: tap a day for its moments, lessons and hours
• Share photos in a Karvo design, or straight to an Instagram story: try Photobomb, where Kevin pops up in the corner with your photo in his goggles
• Everything is saved to a Karvo album in your gallery

ON YOUR PC AND IPHONE TOO
Open karvo.rxsas.it on your computer or iPhone and link it by scanning a code. While Karvo is open on your phone, your computer or iPhone shows your lessons, calendar, statistics and badges, and every change you make there is saved on your phone. They talk end-to-end encrypted.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your lessons live on your phone: no account, no ads, no tracking
• Up to 3 backups inside the app, plus export and import as CSV
• With your phone's Google backup on, your lessons come back on a new phone

HERE TO HELP
• Something wrong, or an idea? Report a problem from Settings, and read the answers and reply under My reports
• A quick tour on the first start shows you around, and What's new tells you what changed after each update

A NEW LOOK
The night sky at first light over the mountains, with Kevin the penguin for company. Prefer something plainer? Switch to the Classic theme.

Everything is free: no Pro plan, no in-app purchases.
