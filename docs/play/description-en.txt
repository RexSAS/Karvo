Log your ski lessons in seconds and always know your hours and what you're owed.
---
Karvo is the lesson log for ski instructors.

You're paid by the hour, and at the end of the month the hours have to add up. With Karvo you log each lesson the moment it ends, then see your month, your season and your pay at a glance. No account, no sign-up, nothing to set up.

LOG A LESSON IN SECONDS
• Quick durations (1h, 1h 30m, 2h, 3h) or type your own
• Student names suggested from your past lessons
• A note for each lesson, like "Kids 6 and 9" or "Lesson in English"
• Lessons grouped by month, with each month's total

YOUR HOURS AND YOUR PAY
• Your season's hours, compared with last season at the same date
• Earnings from your hourly rate, hidden until you tap the eye, with fingerprint, face or PIN if you want
• Tick the months the school has paid, and see what is still to be paid
• Statistics: lessons this month, average lesson, students, the last 6 months, your busiest weekdays and a podium of your top students

CALENDAR AND REMINDERS
• Add events to any day, like "Ski school meeting 17:00"
• Karvo reminds you at 07:30 on the day
• The next 3 days' events right on the Lessons page

MOMENTS
• Tap for a photo, hold for a video of up to 10 seconds: up to 3 moments a day
• Your season as a photo calendar: tap a day for its moments, lessons and hours
• Share photos in a Karvo design, or straight to an Instagram story
• Everything is saved to a Karvo album in your gallery

ON YOUR PC TOO
Open karvo.rxsas.it on your computer and link it by scanning a code. While Karvo is open on your phone, the PC shows your lessons, calendar and statistics on a big screen, and every change you make there is saved on your phone. Phone and PC talk end-to-end encrypted.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your lessons live on your phone: no account, no ads, no tracking
• Up to 3 backups inside the app, plus export and import as CSV
• With your phone's Google backup on, your lessons come back on a new phone

A NEW LOOK
The night sky at first light over the mountains, with Kevin the penguin for company. Prefer something plainer? Switch to the Classic theme.

Everything is free: no Pro plan, no in-app purchases.
In Italian and English.
